Practical help, at your pace

Four ways DavKays can help.

From a useful tool to a system shaped around your business, choose the support that fits your needs.

01 / Ready to use

Ready-made systems

A custom build is not always needed. A ready to use system can offer a solid starting point, with clear guidance for putting it to work.

What’s included

  • A clearly explained system and what it helps you do
  • Setup notes and straightforward instructions
  • A chance to ask questions about fit before you buy
  • Optional setup help, where available

How to get started

Share what needs improvement. DavKays can recommend a suitable option, explain its requirements, and help confirm that it fits.

02 / Made for you

Custom system creation

When existing tools do not fit, DavKays can create a system around the way your business works. The process starts with the problem, then moves to a clear plan, a build, and agreed refinements.

What’s included

  • A discovery conversation about your goals and current workflow
  • A clear scope, timeline, and cost before work begins
  • Build, review, and agreed refinements
  • A handover with guidance so you can use your new system confidently

How to get started

Send a short note about what needs to become easier. The scope and fit of a custom solution can then be discussed.

03 / Support when you need it

Maintenance & support

Systems need care as a business changes. Support can be shaped around the system and the help needed. The exact work and terms are agreed in writing before service begins.

What’s included

  • A review of the system and support needs
  • A written list of the work covered
  • Agreed fees, billing, and cancellation terms
  • Response times, check frequency, and exclusions confirmed before work begins

How to get started

Share the system in use and the support needed. The proposed scope, schedule, response windows, and fees will be confirmed in writing before any maintenance plan starts.

04 / Troubleshooting

Software fixing

Something is broken, slow, or refusing to work as expected? DavKays can troubleshoot software problems, fix supported issues, and help get the setup back into shape.

What is included

  • A conversation about the issue and the software you use
  • Clear next steps before repair work begins
  • Supported fixes, setup help, and a simple explanation of what changed
  • A referral or honest recommendation if the issue needs a different specialist

How to get started

Send the name of the software, what is going wrong, and any error message you see. Please do not send passwords or private account information.
