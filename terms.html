Terms of service

Clear starting terms for using the site and discussing project work.

These starter terms explain how enquiries and service work are handled by DavKays. They apply to use of this website. A separate written proposal or agreement should set the details for each paid project. This is a general starting point, not legal advice; have it reviewed for the laws and business requirements that apply before relying on it.

Using this website

You may use this site to learn about DavKays and make genuine enquiries. Do not misuse the site, attempt to disrupt it, or access systems or information without permission. Website content is provided for general information and may be updated.

Project enquiries and proposals

Sending an enquiry does not commit either side to a project. Before paid work begins, DavKays will discuss the requested outcome and provide a written proposal or agreement describing the work, deliverables, timeline, price, payment schedule, and any support included. Work begins after both sides accept those terms.

Price, payment, and changes

Pricing is discussed according to the project’s needs. The agreed price and payment dates will be confirmed in writing before work starts. If the request changes or new work is added, the timeline and price may also change; the change should be agreed in writing first. Any deposit, late payment, refund, or cancellation terms will be stated in the project agreement.

Review and delivery

The project agreement should explain what the client needs to provide, how progress will be reviewed, and how corrections or refinements are handled. DavKays will make reasonable efforts to meet the agreed scope and schedule. Delays may occur if required information, access, or feedback is not provided on time.

Ownership and third-party software

The project agreement will state who owns or may use the completed work, and when any agreed rights take effect. Unless the agreement says otherwise, third-party software, fonts, images, hosting, and other tools remain subject to their own licences and fees. No professional or product licences are claimed by this website; any relevant licences or credentials will be identified when applicable.

Maintenance and support

Maintenance, response times, updates, and ongoing support are included only when described in the project agreement. A fix or build does not automatically include unlimited future changes or support.

Availability and responsibility

DavKays will take reasonable care when providing agreed work, but cannot promise that a website, software, or third-party service will always be uninterrupted or error-free. Each party remains responsible for its own devices, accounts, backups, and access credentials. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot legally be limited.

Privacy and contact

Information shared through enquiries is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. For questions about these terms, use the Email DavKays link on the Contact page.

Before launch: confirm the legal business name and address to use on agreements, and have the governing law and consumer, payment, cancellation, and liability wording reviewed for the jurisdictions where you operate and serve customers.
