Accessibility

DavKays aims to make this site welcoming and usable for everyone. This statement explains the accessibility features and how to report a barrier.

DavKays aims to make davkays.me accessible to as many people as possible and to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a practical target. This is an intention, not a claim of certification; the site should be reviewed with people and assistive technologies before formal compliance is stated.

What the site provides

  • Semantic page landmarks and a clear heading structure
  • A skip link, keyboard-operable navigation, and visible focus styles
  • Responsive layouts and support for reduced-motion preferences
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and labels for form fields
  • High contrast text and uncluttered page layouts

Known limitations

This is a new site and may have issues that haven’t been identified yet. The homepage photo and web fonts are hosted by third parties and may load differently depending on your connection or settings. The contact form requires an email application to send its prefilled message.

Share accessibility feedback

If you encounter a barrier or would like information in another format, please email info@davkays.me. Please describe the page and what you were trying to do so the issue can be reviewed.

Last reviewed: 15 November 2026. Review this statement and verify the website before launch.
