Good to know

Common questions.

What should I prepare?

Your name and role, a short introduction, a few projects with descriptions, links you want to share, and any optional logo or profile image.

Does the starting price include a domain and hosting?

No. Domain, hosting and third-party provider charges are listed separately and confirmed with the provider in your quotation.

Can you add a contact form?

The starting scope uses an email or WhatsApp enquiry link. A hosted form that submits information to a service or backend is scoped separately.

Can the design be adjusted?

Share the colors, font preferences and content you have in mind. The exact customisation is confirmed in the project scope before work starts.
