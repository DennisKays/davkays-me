Privacy policy

How DavKays handles messages, website preferences, and the services used to run this site.

This is a practical starting policy for the DavKays website. DavKays is currently operated by one person in Zimbabwe. This notice explains the information this website handles today and should be reviewed if the site or services change.

Information you choose to send

The contact form does not submit information to a DavKays server. When you press its button, it opens your email application with a message prepared for you to review and send. If you send that email, the information in it, including your name, reply address, and project details, is received in the email account used by DavKays. You can also choose to contact DavKays by phone or WhatsApp. Those services handle information under their own privacy terms.

How messages are used

Messages are used to reply to enquiries, discuss possible work, and provide services when an agreement is made. Information is not sold. Messages may be kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, provide agreed work, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. Ask to correct or delete information by using the Email DavKays link on the Contact page. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

Website, device, and service data

The site saves your greeting language choice in local storage on your device so it can remember that preference. The site does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. The web host may process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and protect the site; its practices depend on the host selected for deployment.

External services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the homepage image from Unsplash. Your browser may send technical request information to those services. If you follow a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp handles your visit and any messages under its own policies. These services are independent of DavKays.

Children

This website and its services are intended for businesses and adults. Please do not send personal information about a child unless it is necessary and you have authority to share it.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website and services grow. The date at the top shows the latest revision. For a privacy question or request, use the Email DavKays link on the Contact page.

Before launch: confirm your hosting provider’s data practices and check this policy against the privacy laws that apply to your business and customers.
